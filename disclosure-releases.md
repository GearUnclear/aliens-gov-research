## PURSUE — war.gov/UFO

**PURSUE** = *Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters*. Task force stood
up by the Department of War on 2026-05-01; interagency (White House, ODNI, DOE, NASA, FBI,
AARO/Pentagon). Portal: <https://www.war.gov/ufo/>. All five 2026 tranches below were released
there — **not** on aliens.gov.

## Legislative track

- **2026-07-22** — House passes FY2027 NDAA (H.R. 8800) **216–212** ([Roll Call 278](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2026278))
  with **Burlison's UAP Disclosure Act amendment**: permanent NARA UAP Records Collection,
  independent UAP Records Review Board, 25-year presumptive disclosure, contractor coverage.
- **~2026-07-15** — **S.Amdt. 6344** (Schumer/Rounds): revised UAPDA on the Senate side; adds
  SAP-access disclaimers, moves oversight to House Oversight + Senate HSGAC. Senate floor action
  incomplete as of 2026-08-23 → conference.
- Still parked: H.R. 1187 (UAP Transparency Act), H.R. 5060 (UAP Whistleblower Protection Act).

## Oversight bodies created in the window

- **UAP Governance Board** (ODNI + FBI + DoW, first met 2026-06-16) — coordination of
  investigation and declassification under EO 13526. No Federal Register notice or public
  charter located.
- **UAP Science Advisory Council** (2026-06-17, public site 2026-07-01) — chaired by **Avi
  Loeb**, reporting to the Governance Board. Unpaid, unbudgeted, no classified access; members
  largely from Loeb's Galileo Project network (Nolan, Knuth, Szydagis, Gallaudet, Hanson, Lamm…)
  plus professional skeptic **Michael Shermer**.

## The scorecard so far

Across five PURSUE tranches, one AARO annual report, and every document reviewed by Congress
in the window: **the U.S. government's official position is unchanged — no case has produced
verifiable evidence of extraterrestrial technology or beings.** Hundreds of cases remain
formally *unresolved* (insufficient data), which each camp reads its own way. Loeb, chairing
the government's own science council, says nothing released so far is convincing as non-human;
Grusch alleges the real records never entered the pipeline; skeptics (West, Novella) find the
released cases dissolve under analysis; disclosure activists (Greer) call the process itself a
controlled dead end.
