SNAPSHOT 2026-08-23 PREV 2026-04-27 REGISTERED 2026-03-17

What this is. A research dossier on aliens.gov and alien.gov — two domains registered by the Executive Office of the President in March 2026, in the wake of President Trump's February 2026 directive to release federal UAP/UFO records — and on what that directive actually produced. Compiled from public records, DNS forensics, and news reporting. Nothing here is from a privileged source.

THE TWIST (MAY 2026): aliens.gov launched — and it isn't about UFOs. The domain redirects to an immigration-enforcement dashboard styled like the X-Files. The actual UAP records went to a different domain entirely: war.gov/UFO, under a program called PURSUE. Both stories are documented below.

§1 STATUS BOARD

aliens.gov
LIVE since 2026-05-28 — redirects to whitehouse.gov/aliens, an ICE immigration-enforcement dashboard ("THEY WALK AMONG US"). Hosts no UAP records.
alien.gov
UNOBSERVED — same infrastructure as aliens.gov; current HTTP behavior undocumented by any reporting. The last open surface of the original story.
UAP records
LIVE at war.gov/UFO — PURSUE program, five release tranches 2026-05-08 → 2026-08-07, ~375–379 files total.
Official position
Unchanged after five releases: no verifiable evidence of extraterrestrial technology or beings (AARO FY2025 annual report, ~2026-07-21). Hundreds of cases formally unresolved.
Legislation
UAP Disclosure Act passed the House inside the FY2027 NDAA, 216–212 (2026-07-22). Senate action pending → conference.
Infrastructure
Both domains: Cloudflare NS + Automattic/WordPress-VIP edge (192.0.66.230), byte-for-byte unchanged April → August. SPF v=spf1 -all, DNSSEC unsigned.

§7 EXPERT PERSPECTIVES

Full analysis in expert-perspectives.md.

  • Avi Loeb — the dossier's March-era outside speculator now chairs the government's UAP Science Advisory Council — and says nothing released so far convinces him of non-human origin ("a distance measurement is worth a thousand words"). His council has no clearances, no budget, no pay; he continues his separate 3I/ATLAS anomaly catalog on Medium.
  • AARO — position unchanged through five tranches and an annual report: no verifiable ET evidence; ~40% of cases unresolved for lack of data.
  • Whistleblowers — Grusch escalated (DIA "slush fund" allegation + IG complaint); Elizondo toured 18 states; the ODNI memo channels future whistleblowers to AARO/PURSUE — not to the public.
  • Skeptics — Shermer joined the council while betting against disclosure, then apologized to Grusch; Mick West fact-checks tranches on Metabunk; Novella: the drops add dots without connecting them. From the other flank, Greer calls the whole process a controlled dead end.
  • The convergence test — every camp agreed in March that value depends on what gets posted. What got posted made aliens.gov worthless to all of them simultaneously; the same test now applies to war.gov tranche by tranche.
